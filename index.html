NESS WORLDWIDE · PRODUCT DETAILS

ARKARIX

X-Ray-Based Intelligent Threat Recognition and Operator Training System

National-standard training AI-assisted analysis NXCT-compliant certification
01

About the Platform

Arkarix is an integrated platform that enables X-ray security screening operators to receive training in accordance with national standards, have their performance measured through AI-assisted analysis and obtain NXCT-compliant certification. The platform was developed for presentation and demonstration to public institutions operating in areas such as Aviation Security, Customs Screening, Corrections and Critical Infrastructure.

02

Technical Scope

The System provides end-to-end support for screening personnel working at institutions and facilities that use X-ray security equipment. It covers candidate selection, simulation-based training, the assessment and certification of image-recognition proficiency through the Threat Image Projection (TIP) method, and continuous development through AI-assisted performance analysis.

03

Adaptable Architecture

Covering design, development, testing, security validation, deployment, training, maintenance and support, the System is not limited to a single type of institution. Its flexible architecture is designed to adapt to a wide range of X-ray screening environments.
